## Key finding today

On the full 145-row set, Baseline 1 (bandpass) and Baseline 2 (supervised
NMF) were nearly tied. On the 36-row **valid** subset — the rows where
genuine separation is actually possible — the picture changes:

| | SDR heart | SDR lung |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline 1 (bandpass) | 5.61 ± 3.68 dB | 3.94 ± 3.57 dB |
| Baseline 2 (NMF) | 3.78 ± 3.17 dB | −0.44 ± 3.82 dB |

The zero-training filter **beats** the learned, leakage-safe NMF
reproduction by ~1.8 dB (heart) and ~4.4 dB (lung) — a bigger and more
one-sided gap than the near-tie on the full set suggested. Not what a naive
reading of the reproduced paper would predict; discussed (not resolved) in
the report.
